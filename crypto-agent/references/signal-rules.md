# Signal rules

## Long-term
- VC round > $50M in infrastructure/RWA/institutional infra
- Multiple reputable sources mention same project/sector
- Regulatory clarity favorable for the sector
- On-chain/metrics growth over 90 days

## Short-term
- Sudden volume spikes on low-cap altcoins
- New listing announcements at major exchanges
- Narrative acceleration: X mentions, Perplexity trending queries
- Funding round announcement with immediate product usecase

## Confidence levels
- high: 3+ sources, consistent direction, clear catalyst
- medium: 2 sources or partial consistency
- low: single source, weak catalyst

## Risk flags
- wash-trade-like volume patterns
- team/ tokenomics red flags if visible in sources
- regulatory warnings in top results
